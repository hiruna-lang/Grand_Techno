A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WANT TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a restaurant reservation?

We recommend checking availability before your visit, especially for groups and special occasions. Send your preferred date, time and number of guests with your inquiry.

What are the opening hours?

Opening hours are to be confirmed. Please check directly with Grand Techno before planning your visit.

Can the leisure area be booked for private functions?

Please send details of your event, preferred date and guest count. The team can confirm which spaces are available and the arrangements that may be possible.

Is parking available?

Parking arrangements are to be confirmed by the venue. Please include any parking or accessibility needs in your inquiry.
